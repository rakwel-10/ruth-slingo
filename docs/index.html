Ruth Slingo

Hi, I'm Ruth. I believe everyone deserves to know what's possible with the money they already have.

My mission is to help people see the whole picture, understand their options, and create a financial plan that actually fits their life.

I'm one of nine kids, raised by parents who were ministers. With a family that big, there wasn't a lot of extra. Hand-me-downs were normal, and as a little girl, getting 50 cents for a chocolate milk at school felt like a big deal. Name-brand cereal or Fruit Roll-Ups? That was a treat.

We knew how to make things work with what we had. But I didn't grow up learning how to save, invest, or build wealth. There simply wasn't extra money to practice with.

That stayed with me.

I spent more than 25 years as an interior designer, learning how to look at the whole picture, work with what was already there, and see possibilities other people might miss.

Funny enough, that's exactly how I approach finances today.

I help families look at everything together: debt, protection, retirement, taxes, college planning, income, and the money they've already worked so hard to build. I've helped people find ways to cut their scheduled debt payoff time in half without adding more to their monthly budget, simply by helping their money work differently.

And that same philosophy shapes how I coach agents and entrepreneurs. I ask questions, connect the dots, get creative, and help people see opportunities they may not have seen on their own.

Because I know what it feels like to grow up without a financial roadmap.

Today, I get to help other people create one.
